# Syncr 2.4.0

Syncr 2.4 makes **the schedule list easier to work with**: search it,
filter it by kind, worker, group and status, sort it by next or last run,
and see at a glance what each schedule runs and which ones need attention.
Put schedules in **groups** and list them by group, worker, kind or status,
and **act on several at once**: run, pause, resume, move or delete a
selection, or a whole group. Groups only organize the list: nothing changes
for how schedules run.

## Upgrade

- **Controller:** back up the database, then pull the new image (`docker
  compose pull && docker compose up -d`) or replace the binary
  ([running the controller](../deployment.md#upgrading)). One database
  migration runs at start: 00014 adds the table of schedule groups and a
  column for a schedule's group, empty for every schedule already there. No
  new setting.
- **Downgrading** to 2.3 means restoring the database backup made before
  the upgrade, as for every release ([upgrading](../upgrade.md)). The down
  script of 00014 drops the groups; every schedule stays, in no group.
- **Workers:** nothing changes for them; 2.3 workers keep working without
  an upgrade.

`gh attestation verify <file> --owner Zeptiny`. Images are tagged `2.4.0`,
`2.4`, `2` and `latest`, for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64.

## What's new

### The schedule list

- **What each schedule runs**, under its name: the kind's icon, then the
  two locations of a transfer or a two-way sync, or the restic job and its
  repository. When it runs and its time zone share one column.
- **Status buttons** count your schedules and narrow the list: **Needs
  attention** (paused by Syncr, or on with a last run that was missed,
  could not start, failed or was interrupted), **On** and **Paused**.
- **Search** in names, descriptions, groups, times, remotes, paths,
  repositories, workers and labels, and **filters** by kind, by the worker
  or label a schedule runs on, and by group.
- **Sort** by name, next run or last run.
- **Group by** group, worker, kind or status, or not at all. Sections fold,
  and the browser remembers which.
- The filters, the sort and the grouping are in the page's address.

### Groups

- Put schedules in **groups** from the list (**Move to group**) or the
  schedule form (**Group**); create them on the way. A schedule is in one
  group or none; groups are private to you, like your schedules.
- A group's menu runs, pauses or resumes all its schedules, starts a new
  schedule in it, and renames or deletes it. Deleting a group keeps its
  schedules, in no group.
- A schedule's page shows its group.

### Several schedules at once

- Tick schedules (or a whole section) and **Run now**, **Pause**,
  **Resume**, **Move to group** or **Delete** them together. One that fails
  does not stop the others, and the message names it.

### API and metrics

- `ScheduleService` has `ListScheduleGroups`, `CreateScheduleGroup`,
  `RenameScheduleGroup`, `DeleteScheduleGroup` and `MoveSchedules`
  (up to 500 schedules, all or none, without a version), and a schedule's
  `groupId` ([scripting the API](../api.md#schedule-groups)).
- `syncr_schedule_info` has a `group` label, empty for none
  ([metrics](../metrics.md#schedules)). The other schedule series are
  unchanged.
- Moving a schedule, or deleting its group, changes the schedule's
  `version`: an `UpdateSchedule` with the version read before fails with
  `ABORTED`.
- `UpdateSchedule` replaces the whole input, so a script that sends no
  `groupId` takes the schedule out of its group; send back the one
  `GetSchedule` returns.

## Known limitations

- Groups organize schedules only: remotes, repositories and jobs have no
  groups, and the job list cannot be filtered by a schedule's group.
- The list is searched, filtered and sorted in the browser, from every
  schedule you have; it is meant for tens to a few hundred schedules.
- Groups are per user: an administrator cannot organize other users'
  schedules, and a link to a filtered list shows another user nothing of
  your groups.
- Transfers, checks, the dashboard and users have no guide yet, so their
  pages have no help link.
- Everything listed for [2.3.0](v2.3.0.md#known-limitations) still applies.
